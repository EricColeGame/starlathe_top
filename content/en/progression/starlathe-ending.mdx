## Starlathe Ending FAQ

**How long does it take to reach the starlathe ending?**
It varies enormously with playstyle. Players who beeline the main objective finish far faster than those who explore, complete side content, and upgrade thoroughly. Rather than chasing a number, judge your readiness by whether the map's remaining objectives have narrowed to the final goal.

**Is the starlathe ending missable?**
In most progression games of this type, the ending itself isn't missable — you can almost always trigger it eventually. What can be missed are the variations tied to optional content and choices made earlier. Keep manual saves before major decisions if you want flexibility.

**Can I keep playing after the credits?**
That depends on the game's post-game structure. Many titles drop you back before the final trigger, letting you clean up optional content. If yours doesn't, reload an earlier save rather than starting over from scratch.

**Do my choices really change the starlathe ending?**
Community reports suggest they do, at least in framing and emphasis. The differences are often subtle rather than dramatic, so don't expect a completely different game. If you want to compare outcomes, save before the point of no return and replay the trigger.

**What's the biggest mistake players make?**
Rushing. Arriving at the finale with unspent upgrades and an empty inventory is the most common reason players report the last stretch as unfair. A little preparation beforehand changes the entire experience.
